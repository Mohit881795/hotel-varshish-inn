Welcome to Hotel Varshish Inn

A Warm Welcome in the Heart of Bhopal

Located in MP Nagar, one of Bhopal's most convenient neighbourhoods, Hotel Varshish Inn offers comfortable accommodation, warm hospitality and easy access to the city's business and leisure destinations.

The hotel welcomes guests with a distinctive Romanian-themed facade and a beautiful, grand reception area. A travel desk and a comfortable waiting lounge are available to make your visit smooth and pleasant — whether you are travelling for business or leisure.

  • Convenient location in MP Nagar, Bhopal
  • Distinctive Romanian-themed facade
  • Grand reception and comfortable waiting lounge
  • Travel desk for assistance during your stay
